HTML List Demonstration

In HTML, both <ul> (unordered list) and <ol> (ordered list) are used to display lists. The main difference is that:

  • <ul> shows itmes with bullets
  • <ol> shows items with numbers or letters (1, 2, 3.... or A, B, C... or a, b, c.... or I, II, III... or i, ii, iii...
